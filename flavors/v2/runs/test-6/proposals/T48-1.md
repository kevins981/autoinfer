# T48-1 (research, leader-48): seed report. One characterization and no novel direction that survives napkin math. No grant requested.

Team T48, leader-48. GPU used: 1.3 of the 20-min seed (jobs 419, 421, 424). Sparring: sparring/T48-1 (6 turns).

## What I found (bank/T48-001)
The 4090's transparent hardware compression (cuMemCreate, COMP_GENERIC) is real, and its rule is now mapped.
- Ratios come in steps: 1x, ~2x, ~3.4x and ~8.6x.
  - ~8.6x needs all-zero 128 B blocks.
  - ~3.4x needs constant or 16 B-periodic blocks.
  - ~2x behaves like base + narrow delta: at most ~2 of 8, 6 of 16 or 12 of 32 bits per element.
- The rule is not entropy-based. Real FP8 exponent bits, about 32% of their byte's bits as information, don't compress.
- So no exact layout of high-entropy data (weights, lm_head, GDN state, KV) can beat dense packing through this path.
- The real checkpoint has 0 zero or narrow 128 B blocks.
- T49-002 found the same headline one job earlier, independently. My record adds the rule and why it closes the door.

## What I examined and killed before using GPU time (local_kb/leader-48.md)
- Rounding-aware all-reduce (send only the bits that can change bf16(h+s)): ambiguity is ~2·delta/ulp(h), so nearly
  every bit is needed.
- Lending the 5 GiB KV reservation to weight replicas: the reservation isn't idle at k=3, because GDN spec-state pages
  fill much of it. The replicas I costed (mixer out_proj plus an FP8 all-gather of the 3072-wide pre-projection, or a
  row-split MLP for prompt rows) give ~1-3%. They are known layout choices, overlapping T45's min-cut and T46.
- Single-microbatch PP with prefill in the bubbles: decode 0.65x TP (KB112) loses more than the prefill saves. T49-001
  simulates this now.
- CPU/AMX prefill of short prompts: the closed loop charges the idle slot time, net ~+2%, and the build is large.
- A spatial prefill lane: zero-sum on HBM bytes, because the lane re-reads the weights, not only on SMs.
- Drafter replication or data-parallel drafting: a wash on bytes.

## Where I think the open value is (for the manager, not a request)

- I found nothing novel in my lane that the open teams (T45/T46/T47/T49) aren't already covering.
