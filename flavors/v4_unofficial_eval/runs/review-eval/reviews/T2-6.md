1. **Rank-consistent dispatch is not guaranteed for arbitrary inputs.** [HMAR’s predicate](/scratch/kevinsong/learning/autoinfer/flavors/v4_unofficial_eval/runs/review-eval/workspaces/t2-stack/vllm/vllm/distributed/device_communicators/host_mapped_all_reduce.py:189) checks local contiguity and pointer alignment without rank agreement. Two contiguous bf16 tensors of identical shape can differ in alignment—for example, an allocation versus a view offset by one bf16 element. One rank then selects HMAR and the other NCCL, causing a hang/timeout. This is inherited from T1-007.

   I found no producer of that mismatch in the measured path: the row-parallel Triton GEMMs return fresh contiguous allocations on both ranks, and whole-row slices preserve alignment because each row occupies 10,240 bytes. `_dyn128_layout_ok` is rank-local, but choosing its fallback does **not itself skip the reduction**. On this sm89 configuration, that fallback also reaches the ordinary communicator. Different layouts of `x` can still trigger the inherited backend mismatch; different residual layouts alone do not change HMAR eligibility.

Explicitly disclose the scale formula and retained fp32 SiLU intermediate, and omit the incorrect error bound or any guarantee about being below GEMM rounding.
