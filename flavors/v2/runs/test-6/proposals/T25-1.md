Team T25 (explore), leader-25. Champion: T14-004, 956.6 tok/s. Bar: 1004.4 (+5%).

## Why a package, and why now
I read T2-T24 and KB1-KB81 and re-costed the large directions from first principles before writing this.
I agree with them: decode sits on coupled HBM, PCIe and SM floors. The arithmetic (CPU only, from KB47/KB79/T19 L2):
- Decode HBM floor: ~15.4 GB/rank/step (weights 12.15, lm_head 0.99, GDN state ~1.2, drafter ~1) is about 16 ms.
- The serial SM chain: GEMM MMA ~8 ms at 100% of peak, AR 5.6, other 6.7. That is about 21 ms even with a perfect GEMM.
- Measured: 25.3 ms. Closing that gap needs a warm GEMM well beyond Marlin's, plus continuous L2 streaming. T8, T17 and T24 failed at the first part. I don't propose it.
- Prefill at the bf16 roof. Exact INT8/FP8 slicing is 2.25x or more slower (T18).

**What is left is the mixed-step gap**, and it is not small in total:
- Mixed steps are 40% of wall (KB77).
- Floor estimate for that time: 104K prefill rows plus 370 x 48 decode rows at 0.167 ms/row (146 TF, GPU0), about 20 s.
- Real mixed time is about 37 s.
- So ~16 s, about 17% of wall, sits above the floor.

T19's critical-path attribution splits this ~17% into Marlin rank skew and padding, exposed comm, glue and idle. Each piece is ~1-2%, which is why every team called them exploit items. This run has **no exploit team** (config.md: 2 explore, 0 exploit), so nobody harvests them. T14 showed how to land one: in situ, then ABBA, then official. I propose to do that, item by item, starting with the largest one nobody has built: the rank rebalance.

## Roadmap (each stage lands or dies on its own; every landing is an in-situ ABBA, then an official)
**S1. GPU1-aware MLP rebalance for compute-bound steps** (new build; sized by T9 and T18, never built).
- Facts:
  - GPU0's SM clock is locked at 2235 MHz. GPU1 runs at 2520-2655 MHz (T14-004 official gpu_clocks.csv, both trials).
  - Compute-bound kernels are 15-17% slower on GPU0 (KB8).
  - Mixed-step Marlin: rank 0 131 ms vs rank 1 113 ms at 400-800 rows. Rank 1's extra exposed comm (46 vs 29 ms) is waiting for rank 0 (T19 L2 tables).
- Design:
  - GPU1 holds a duplicate of a fraction delta (~7%) of GPU0's MLP intermediate columns: gate_up columns plus the matching down rows, ~0.6 GB.
  - In steps with padded M >= threshold (compute-bound), GPU0 runs gate_up/down on its N-/K-subrange without that slice. GPU1 runs its own half plus the slice. The AR sum is unchanged: every K-slice is computed exactly once, so the result is a reordering at the <=1-ulp class, the same as the token split.
  - Decode steps keep the even split: GPU1 uses only its own subrange, so decode bytes don't change.
  - Memory: T5-005's two-slot GDN route B (bitwise, 128K pass) frees GDN spec blocks, so the KV pool can shrink by the slice size at equal admitted capacity. The fallback is to trim the graph pool (2.46 GiB/rank captured).
- Optional extension: a small decode shift (~1-2%) toward GPU1. Rank 1 waits ~0.5 ms per decode step (T19: comm 6.1 vs 5.6 ms).

**S2. Small mixed-step path (pt < 256, ~220 steps/trial).**
- These steps run 67 ms vs 23.5 ms for decode (T23-001).
- Excess per step: Marlin +20.5 ms, comm +16, 587 vs 46 glue launches (+2.4 ms), chunked-GDN prefill +2.3, in-span idle +3.1.
- Target: the glue and idle (~5 ms/step), via fused GDN-prefill index glue and fewer eager breaks in the breakable graph.

**S3. Lifecycle.**
- Late scheduling of step N+2, so a freed slot refills at N+2 instead of N+3. Dead slot-time is 1.1% (T19 L1).
- Drain adaptive k: k=3-4 while <= 12/9 requests remain, keeping M <= 48. Uses T5's two-slot GDN and adaptive plumbing.

**S4 (optional, only if S1-S3 land). Budgeted verification K_MAX=3 at M=48** on T5-006's built plumbing.
- First isolate T5's unexplained mixed-step slowdown on the adaptive path.
