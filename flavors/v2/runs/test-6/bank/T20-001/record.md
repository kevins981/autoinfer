  conclusion: |
    Interpretation: Atomicity assumption held (0 mismatches over 5e9 words with skew both ways and HBM load, 8 B units, 16 B vector stores/loads with independent tags). The protocol works and costs only 2 % bytes, but it does NOT reach the gate: the standalone AR drops 38.4 -> 33.1 us (-5.3), not to the 26-28 estimated (SM W||R floor 25 us + ~1.5 us launch; 7 us above floor), and in the chain it saves only 2-3 us/AR.
    Why the chain saving is small: with the prefetch blocks the AR kernel lasts ~69 us in the chain (kernel end 69-70 us vs 32-35 us for the same AR without prefetch; the prefetch of the next layer's ~88 MB of weights takes ~65 us of HBM time and keeps the kernel alive; the champion has the same structure). Shortening the transport by x us shortens the prefetch window by x, and KB41's credit (~0.4-0.6 ms per ms of window) gives most of it back: 5.3 us standalone saving -> ~2-3 us net. Consistent with T16's lost-prefetch-window effect. T_3 vs Tpf_3: prefetch is worth ~11 us/AR in the chain.
    Why 33 not 25: the writer needs 14-15 us to issue its stores (encode + smem pack + 16 B stores from 48 warps), the reader finishes ~16 us after (30-34 us total from reader start in chain, of which the peer's start skew is part). Not explored: more writer parallelism with fewer polling warps (G=15/30 are slower: 43.7 us, probably 240+ warps polling over PCIe contend with the writes), separate writer and reader warp counts, interleaving encode and store (store as soon as the first words of a group are complete), 2 groups in flight in the writer. Maybe 3-5 us more standalone, which would still be ~1.5-3 us in chain after the prefetch credit loss: not enough for >= 6.
    Verdict: gate (>= 6 us/AR chain saving on both ranks) FAILED: +1.9/+2.3 (G=3), +2.8/+3.2 (G=2). Stop line suggested: even a floor-level 25 us AR would save ~(38.4-26.5)*0.5-0.6 = 6-7 us in the chain.
