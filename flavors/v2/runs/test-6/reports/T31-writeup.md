## What was found (Observed -> Interpretation)
1. **L2 facts (T31-001, jobs 236/239/246/263).** Plain ldg reads from L2 run at ~3.4 TB/s (4.4-4.7 TB/s on 64 MB after real-load prefetch) up to ~72 MB working sets; T26's 1.29 TB/s "warm" (KB83) was its kernel. `prefetch.global.L2::evict_last` hints are largely dropped under load (~40% of 64 MB lands; 20-30 MB per real AR window with 8 blocks, 11-15 with 16). Real loads with an evict_last policy (ptxas deletes dead loads: store to a device sink) land 100% but at ~0.85 TB/s: 32 MB per ~40 us AR window at +2 us stretch (16 blocks); beyond that ~1.2 us of AR per MB. In the full 16-layer chain the real-load mode LOSES (AR2 prefetches two ranges: +26-36 us), hint stays default; useful hint cap ~48-64 MB on AR1 (saturates at 64).
2. **Kernel (T31-002, jobs 243..287).** Register-direct e4m3 m16n8k32 on the champion's raw Marlin fp8 words (T29's PI fragment), tokens on m16, per-K-group A ring with named barriers, fp32 promotion per 128-K block, deterministic. 1 ulp vs T29's bs_gemm, 0 spills. GPU0 M=48 cold|warm vs Marlin: qkvz 41.4|25.7 vs 44.6|40.3; down 46.2|28.2 vs 52.7|45.4; gdn_out 18.8|15.0 vs 25.1|21.3; attn_qkv 36.6|24.4 vs 44.7|38.5. Per forward -5% cold / -15% warm; cold advantage varies by job (-6..-27%). v1 (persistent stream-K) paid 12-19 us/launch of fixed cost; v2 ~6.5 us (launch 1.3 + prologue 3 + tail 0.4-3.4); nothing cheap left.
3. **gate_up is a wall (jobs 278/281).** 89 MB > 72 MB L2. Both kernels sit at ~82-88 us for any residency from 30 to 58 MB, contiguous or interleaved along K; evict_first on cold loads gives 3-4%; a reader check shows 86-107% of the resident lines survive the GEMM, so eviction is not the cause: the cold stream (weights-only 48-63 us) and a poorly overlapped MMA phase (53 us at a 2.1-wave layout) are. My forecast error: I assumed resident bytes are read at the L2 rate independently of the cold part and that residency scaled with the request; the AR window caps residency at ~32 MB and the phases do not overlap into a max.
4. **In situ (T31-003).** Chain (hint 48 MB, Marlin on gate_up): -16.8 us/layer gross. Smoke +1.3% (1 pair). ABBA +2.25% (job 285) with a systematic TTFT shift (+15 ms p50); cause: 12 us of Python per linear call in the dispatch (workspace requirement recomputed per call), ~2.3 ms/step on eager mixed steps; after a per-layer-resolved rewrite (3.3 us) the ABBA is +2.75% (job 290) with no TTFT shift. In-situ transfer of the chain prediction ~1:1 for decode.
