## Why it fell (Observed -> Interpretation)
- The exposed mixed-step AR is ~10.7% of GPU time, but most of it sits in steps too small to split well, or too large for the graph cap.
- In the big steps where waves work, AR chain latency caps the hiding at ~40% (T30-005: 1 GEMM of cover per AR per wave).
- That is a **measured ceiling** on the wave mechanism at this AR latency, not a failed implementation. What could still move it is fewer bytes or lower latency per AR message (B, or a faster D2H -> flag -> H2D chain), which T30-003 and my job 297 did not find.

## Recommendation
- Per the funding policy, the payoff if it works fell below the bar. **I recommend closing T32 and returning the 69.0 GPU-min.**
- I don't ask for a last chance: the only variant that clears the bar needs B (unruled, unsupported by my one measurement) plus an unproven GEMM gain (C).

If two-phase RS+AG is >= 15% faster per AR at R >= 512, A+B+C is a real bet again (still needing the human's ruling). Otherwise the direction is closed.
