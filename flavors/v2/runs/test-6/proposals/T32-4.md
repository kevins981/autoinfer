# T32-4 (leader-32): results of the T32-3 tests, and recommendation

GPU used: 0.2 of 20 (jobs 296, 297). Record: bank/T32-001.

## Tests run
**Test 1 (CPU; host cost as the blocker of mixed-step waves).** T30-005's own trace already answers it (bank/T30-005, agents/leader-30.w2/trace, job 294):
- Host-free replay of a captured step at n=734: joined 131.0 ms, 2 waves 123.6, 3 waves 116.2, 4 waves ~124. AR-off floor 88-93.
- Even with zero host cost, waves hide only ~16 of the 42.7 ms exposed AR. The gaps are AR dependency latency: each wave has ~1 GEMM of cover per AR. Copy/compute contention is absent (copy rate 20.7 vs 21.3 GB/s, GEMMs unslowed).
- In-server, the eager wave step is host-bound: 189-212 ms wall for k=2, 923 ms for k=3.
- Interpretation: removing host cost is necessary but the replay itself caps the gain at -11% of a big step. Big steps (pt >= 209) are ~28% of GPU time, so the ceiling is ~-3.4% of time. Making the chunked-GDN prefill core cheap or graph-captured is a large build, not attempted by anyone.

**Test 4 (CPU; mixed-step GEMM headroom).**
- From the node trace (agents/leader-32/seed/big.py): a pt=308 step runs 512 bs_gemm calls on two concurrent chunk streams. GEMM union is 48.8 ms (pt 209-400 median) vs a 29.3 ms compute floor (353 rows x 24.2 GFLOP/row/GPU at GPU0's 291 TF e4m3 MMA peak, KB69): 60%. At pt~700: 79.1 vs ~58 floor, 73%.
- T29's standalone table (agents/leader-29.w2/gemm/summary2.md) puts the practical ceiling: gate_up 192 TF at M=256, 236 TF at M=1024 (66-81% of peak). cuBLASLt, which lacks the mandatory per-128-K promotion, reaches ~218 TF at M=256 (75%).
- The token split runs each GEMM at M/2 (per forward 2 x ~25 ms at M~176 vs ~42 ms joined at M~352). But T30 arm C (no split) was not faster at pt 209-400 (93.5 vs 93.6), because the split's AR cover pays it back.
- Interpretation: realistic GEMM headroom ~6-12% of mixed GEMM time, ~0.45-0.9 s per 200 prompts, **~+1.2-2.4% e2e**, assumed achievable. T29 stopped tuning at 1.04-1.11x cuBLASLt.

**New GPU test (jobs 296/297, bank/T32-001): RS (bf16) + owner add/quant + AG (e4m3 + scales) vs AR, copy-engine transport, R=128..1024.**
- AR slope 55 us/MB (18-21 GB/s, consistent). RS+AG slope 77 us/MB on 24% fewer bytes: total time ~8% higher at every R.
- The harness has a ~1.5 ms constant and ~65 us per extra piece from cross-device event signalling in one process, so it overstates dependency latency vs the champion's device-polled host flags.
- Interpretation: no support for the byte cut turning into time. With realistic ~5-20 us signalling, the RS+AG ceiling is the byte model: <= 24% of the exposed mixed AR, ~+2.5% e2e. Unproven; it also needs a ruling (FP8 activation bytes on the link) and handling of in_proj_ba's bf16 input.

## The only stack that reaches the bar, with its parts
Base: 200-prompt T30 profile (job 249/254), GPU time ~37.8 s at T31-003 decode speed. Exposed mixed-step AR ~4.0 s (10.7%): 0.29 s (pt 1-80) + 0.42 s (81-208) + 0.91 s (209-400) + 2.0 s (401-1000) + 0.41 s (>1000), from T30-002 class medians.

| part | saving if it works | status |
|---|---|---|
| A. host-free 3-wave schedule in big steps (needs graph-captured / host-cheap GDN prefill core) | 39% of big-step exposed AR = 1.30 s (3.4%) | replay **measured** (job 294); in-server **assumed**: host cost fix unbuilt, T30 closed it on host cost |
| B. RS + fp8 AG on the remaining mixed ARs | 24% of what's left = 0.65 s (1.7%) | **assumed**; my harness (job 297) did not realise it; needs a validity ruling |
| C. mixed W8A8 GEMM to ~70-75% of MMA peak | 0.45-0.9 s (1.2-2.4%) | gap **measured** (trace + T29 table); achievability **assumed** after T29 stopped at cuBLASLt parity |

- Payoff if all three work: 2.4-2.85 s of 37.8 s, **+6.8-8.1%** over T31-003. That clears 1143.3, but only if all three hit their ceilings.
- Central estimate: about **+1.5-2%** (A at ~25% odds, B ~25%, C ~30%).
- Chance of reaching >= +5%: **~5-10%**.
- GPU needed: ~150-200 min across three independent large builds (A: GDN core capture with dummy-sequence padding plus a k-wave scheduler; B: a device-flag two-phase AR in the champion's DMA path; C: new W8A8 tiles or stream-K for M 128-400).

## Recommendation
**Close T32.** No single measured item reaches +5%. The only stack that clears the bar on paper needs three large builds, each to hit its ceiling. Its largest part (A) is the direction T30 measured and closed. My one new measurement for B (job 297) did not support it.

If the manager prefers a small low-odds bet over closing, the cheapest decisive step is ~20 GPU-min, with a kill gate:
1. Build the two-phase AR on the champion's device-flag DMA path and microbench it at R=256/734/1024. Gate: >= 15% faster than the current AR at R >= 512.
2. If it passes: in a 4-layer chain harness, measure whether the GDN prefill core can be captured with a dummy padding sequence, bitwise equal state and host time per step.

Stop at the first failed gate.
