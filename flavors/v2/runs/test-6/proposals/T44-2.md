# T44-2: the copy-engine all-reduce waits on its own doorbells, not on the hardware

Team T44 (research), leader-44. Evidence: bank/T44-003 (job 403), bank/T44-002 (job 401). Spar: sparring/ T44-2.

## Finding (measured)
The split mixed steps' two-lane copy-engine AR (VLLM_T9_PINGPONG=1, every step >= 256 tokens) runs at:
- 247-289 us untraced at 300 rows, vs ~165-185 us for its correctness-required critical path;
- 438-468 us at 700 rows, vs ~330-390.

KB104's "631 us floor" was a hybrid transport's, and the "20-35 us copy-engine start latency" (KB74) was taken as structural.
The per-piece timeline shows instead that ~80% of the gap is ordering the code imposes:
1. The doorbell (cuStreamWriteValue32 on the D2H stream) completes 13-60 us after its D2H, behind in-flight posted writes,
   and blocks the next piece's D2H. The D2H engine is idle half the time. (~60% of the gap)
2. The flag reset sits before each H2D. (~15%)
3. The ack is written before the join. (~5%; more at 700 rows)

What remains is bytes at the duplex rate plus one piece's latency, i.e. the bound. Copy-engine hardware latency isn't the
limit on this path.

**For future teams:** stream-ordered doorbells (write-value / wait-value) on a data stream serialize behind that stream's
posted writes. Signal on a separate stream gated by an event. This applies to any CE-pipelined transport here, including
the ones closed earlier on latency grounds (KB74/KB100).

## Change (bitwise; scheduling only)
In dma_staged_ar.cu (pipelined path):
- (a) A per-lane doorbell stream does WriteValue32(F[p]) after waiting on an event recorded after D2H p. It joins back via
  an event, so the call stays graph-capturable.
- (b) The F resets move after the last H2D, before the ack.
- (c) The join is recorded before the ack write.

Same bytes and same fp32 add, so outputs are bitwise identical.

## Forecast
- **Per split-step AR:** ~-30% at 300 rows (~-75 us), ~-15-20% at 700.
- **Per trial, if fully exposed (KB100 says it is, in big steps):** ~250-350 split steps x 128 ARs x ~70 us ≈ 2.2-3.1 s
  of ~80 s.
- **Transfer to the clock:** microbenchmark gains in mixed steps reach it at ~0.5-0.75x (KB93), so **~+1.5-2.5% e2e**
  central; ceiling ~+3.5%.
- **Odds:** >= +1% ~55%; >= +3% ~15%.

## Risk
Cross-stream ordering: the peer must see D2H p's data before flag p. Event-wait then write-value on another stream should
order it (the CE write is complete when its event fires), but this is exactly the case KB57's negative control exists for.
If the ordering isn't safe, fall back to (b)+(c) only (~20% of the gap).
