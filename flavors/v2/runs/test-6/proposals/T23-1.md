That makes small effects measurable in situ with nsys instead of only with noisy end-to-end ABBAs.

## Roadmap (each item: own record, in-situ nsys A/B gate, then kept or dropped; survivors stacked)
- **M0 (seed, CPU only, running):** worker W1-1 attributes, from T14's existing in-situ nsys (B1), (a) the 3.1 ms between target forwards in decode steps (drafter, sampling, collectives) and (b) the per-kernel excess of small mixed steps over decode, and (c) Marlin per-row cost vs rows. This fixes the item sizes below before any GPU is spent.
- **M1. Drafter step to its byte roof (all ~2,700 steps/trial).** T5-003: a draft step costs 0.8-0.9 ms vs ~0.5 ms of weight bytes; the drafter's 4 host collectives cost 110-183 us per draft step vs ~50 us expected (T12: excess = embed-AR skew + fc gather). Items: kill the embed-AR skew (the embedding AR is the first collective after the target's argmax/accept: overlap or reorder), fuse the fc gather, prefetch the draft lm_head into L2 inside the drafter's own AR windows (the target's KB25 trick, never applied to the drafter). Gate G1: >= 0.25 ms/decode step in nsys A/B.
- **M2. Small mixed steps (R < 256; ~220 of ~370 mixed steps/trial).** (a) Marlin slab padding: the T6 policy computes ceil16(M/S) x S rows (M=105 -> 128 rows, ~18% wasted tensor work at the tensor-bound sizes); use uneven slabs (64 + remainder rounded to 16) or Marlin's 8-row m-block path for the tail. (b) GDN prefill glue and norm/act eager launches (T2-006 list; GDN 4.2 vs 1.55 ms and norm/act 3.3 vs 0.87 ms per small step vs decode). Gate G2: >= 3 ms per small mixed step (rank 0 critical path) in nsys A/B.
- **M3. Slot lifecycle.** (a) late scheduling of step N+2 so a refilled request lands at N+2 instead of N+3 (T19: dead slot-time 1.1%); (b) drain adaptive k (deeper k only when few requests remain, where M is far below 48; T10: ~0.6%). Gate G3: dead slot-time -0.4 points (T19's tracer) with no throughput loss.
- **M4. GPU0/GPU1 rebalance in mixed steps** (GPU0 is clock-locked: rank-0 Marlin 28.3 vs rank-1 24.5 ms in small steps, 131 vs 113 in large; rank 1 waits). Uneven MLP channel split without duplicated weights (T20's variant), sized so decode does not lose (decode is HBM-bound; check per-AR waits). Gate G4: nsys A/B mixed-step span -3% with decode span unchanged (+-0.1 ms). Memory moves between ranks: 128K test.
- **M5. Stack + verify:** one workspace with every surviving item behind switches; same-job ABBA (fresh starts, interleaved, 400 prompts) vs T14-004; GSM8K-50 smoke, 128K; then official. Any single item that shows all-B>all-A on its own can go to official early (T14's path).
