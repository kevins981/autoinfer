## What I put up for attack
Every compute component is at a floor. The only large non-floor bucket is the PCIe all-reduce (~15-17% of wall), and it is locked by hardware (no P2P), by the rules (lossless comm) and by zero-sum comm/compute overlap. Best package ~+4.5-7% if all works, central +2.5%.

- **Decode GEMMs are near the HBM floor at the current weight bytes** (13 GB/GPU/forward at 1 B/weight vs 13.2 ms of GEMM per step; KB79/KB98). The partner accepted the narrow claim. Its caveat: the bytes themselves aren't proven mandatory (lossless floor ~0.82 from 6.55 bits/byte, KB47).

## Beliefs that turned out assumed, or wrong as stated
1. **"Comm/compute overlap is zero-sum": wrong for big mixed steps.** The zero-sum results (KB59/62/70) are decode/SM-driven host traffic. In big steps copy-engine copies run at ~21 GB/s beside the FP8 GEMMs without slowing them (KB100). T30's host-free replays recovered real time: 130.5 -> 123.6 (2 waves) / 116.2 ms (3 waves) vs an 88-93 ms no-AR floor. The obstacle is dependency latency plus host cost in the server, which is an implementation constraint, not a hardware limit. My T32-1/T32-2 wording overstated this.
2. **"Mixed steps are where the time is, evenly": refined.** Of ~15.4 s of mixed GPU time in T30's 200-prompt run, 71% is in BIG steps (pt >= 209, coalesced prompts): 2.9 s at 209-400, 6.5 s at 401-1000, 1.5 s above 1000. Small-step glue/quant items (KB91) touch ~1% of wall. Big steps are ~28% of wall, and their exposed AR is ~9% of wall.
3. **"Mixed GEMMs are at their floor": only library-relative.** Per-call roofline (26 GFLOP/token/GPU, GPU0 e4m3 peak ~291 TF, KB69; split path runs each GEMM per chunk): ~63% of MMA peak at pt~300, ~78% at pt~650. The headroom is plausibly 10-20% at the smaller big steps, ~1% of wall. Not a big lever, but not proven at the floor either.
4. **The apparent "20-ms intercept" fixed overhead (fit 0.109 ms/token + 23.1 ms with AR off) dissolved** under the component breakdown: it's the exposed AR + GEMM efficiency + cores, not a hidden fixed cost. The partner was right that an aggregate max(HBM, compute) roofline understates a sequence of alternating-bound GEMMs.
5. **"2 ARs per layer at these boundaries are mandatory": partly assumed.** RMSNorm needs full rows, which justifies a reduction before each norm. Duplication is memory-blocked (~1 GiB/GPU per weight type), and PP doubles decode HBM. But I haven't traced exactly which values must cross before each consumer.
6. **"Acceptance sets the per-token floor": assumed in general**, measured only for k=3 (neutral, KB95).

## What I now see differently
The most promising direction isn't a deck card or a decode item. It is **host-free layer-pipelined waves for big mixed steps**: T30's direction, with its blocker reframed. T30 closed it as "host-bound in server" (eager chunked GDN core ~1.24 ms host per call, doubled by waves; host execute_model is already ~85% of GPU time at pt 300-400). If the GDN prefill core (and the rest of the per-layer eager glue) were captured in CUDA graphs on a padded size grid, the replay numbers say big steps -11% (3 waves) -> ~+3% e2e. Payoff if it works ~+3-4%, still short of +5% alone. Stacked with my T32-2 #1 (mixed-step FP8 all-gather for out_proj, ~+2%, the same bucket, so partly overlapping), still not clearly over the bar. Neither "a >= 5% package exists" nor "it's impossible" is established. My T32-1/T32-2 conclusion should read "no measured path to +5%", not "nothing can reach +5%".
