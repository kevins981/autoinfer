# T3-1: self-distilled MTP draft head (more tokens per verify step, at the same step cost)

team: T3 (leader-3). Status: proposal. Champion reference: T1-042, 942.2 tok/s.

## Why this lever
Every part of the champion's decode step is at a measured floor (KB45, KB56: Marlin at the HBM roof,
AR at the PCIe floor and compressed, GDN latency-bound). What's left is **how many tokens each
step emits**. Draft quality multiplies the whole step (weights, ARs, GDN, lm_heads) and costs no
extra verify width.
- Observed (T1-042 official trial logs, `bank/T1-042/official/1/trial*/server.log`, SpecDecoding
  metrics): per-position acceptance **0.77 / 0.55** (cumulative), mean acceptance length 2.29-2.35
  (official 2.325). The ceiling at k=2 is 3.0.
- Interpretation: position 2 is weak (conditional 0.71). vLLM runs the single MTP layer
  recursively on its own normalized output (`qwen3_5_mtp.py:189`, `spec_step_idx %
  num_mtp_layers`). The released head was most likely trained on pretraining text for one step
  only. It has never been trained on the target's own chat outputs or on being fed its own
  output. Self-distillation with an unrolled ("training-time test", EAGLE-3 style) loss targets
  exactly this.
- The task_definition ruling of 2026-11-08 allows retraining the MTP head. Verification stays
  exact (greedy verify against the target is unchanged), so outputs stay the target's.

## Data provenance (for the validity review)
- **Prompts:** generated by the target itself. Magpie-style: the chat template up to the user turn,
  then free sampling, plus a self-instruct pass seeded by a generic topic list (code, math,
  writing, science, multilingual, reasoning). No external dataset is used: no ShareGPT, no
  benchmark prompts, no GSM8K. Nothing is filtered or selected by resemblance to the benchmark.
- **Responses:** the target's own temperature-0 outputs (thinking on and off, mixed), served by the
  champion engine. Labels are those tokens, i.e. the target's greedy choices.
- **Model selection** uses only a held-out split of this self-generated data. ShareGPT acceptance is
  measured only at the end (the unofficial bench and the official eval), never used for tuning.

## Roadmap
1. (seed) Pipeline: generate about 150K tokens of self-distillation data. Dump the target's final
   hidden states (the MTP input) with one prefill pass. Measure offline, teacher-forced per-position
   acceptance of the stock head on the held-out split, and check it against the in-server 0.77/0.55
   (validates the pipeline).
2. Scale the data to about 2M tokens (about 20 GPU-min of generation at high concurrency, plus
   about 7 min of hidden-state dump).
3. Fine-tune the MTP layer (fc, norms, decoder layer; embeddings and the 98K FP8 draft lm_head
   frozen) with a 2-step unrolled loss. Training compute is small (about 0.43B trainable params,
   about 9 GFLOP/token for 2 steps, about 2-4 min per epoch on 2 GPUs). Re-quantize to the
   checkpoint's FP8 block format, so memory is unchanged.
4. Load the new head through a separate draft-weights path (the target's weights untouched).
   Same-job ABBA vs T1-042 on the unofficial bench, plus acceptance logs.
5. Options if acceptance rises enough:
   - (a) a dedicated second MTP layer for step 2 (vLLM already indexes `layers[step %
     num_mtp_layers]`; about +0.2 GB/rank, so the 128K test is needed);
   - (b) re-test k=3, which the champion rejected because of acceptance per step time (KB20).
6. Official eval.

## Forecast
Model: tokens/step E = 1 + a1 + a1·a2 (cumulative a2). Throughput ∝ E on the share of wall time
that scales with step count. The prefill compute in mixed steps does not scale with E.
| part | value | status |
|---|---|---|
| Current E, per-position 0.77/0.55 | 2.325 | measured (T1-042 official logs) |
| Step time unchanged (same architecture, same k, same draft lm_head) | 0 cost | assumed (same kernels; check in step 4) |
| Share of wall time that scales with 1/E | about 80% | assumed, from KB31/KB56 (mixed steps 39% of wall, about half of that is prefill compute) |
| E after fine-tuning, if it works: a1 0.83, cumulative a2 0.68 | 2.51-2.65 | assumed (EAGLE-style self-distillation gains; the step-1 pipeline measures the offline baseline) |
| Extra from a step-2 layer or k=3 | 0-3% | assumed, option |

- **Payoff if it works:** E 2.325 → 2.62 (+12.7% tokens/step) × 80% share = **about +10% over
  T1-042 (about 1035 tok/s)**, more if option 5 pays.
- **Central estimate: +4%** (E about 2.44). **About 35% chance of the full payoff.** Main risks:
  - the released head is already near what this layer can do;
  - 2M tokens is too little data;
  - FP8 re-quantization costs acceptance;
  - acceptance on ShareGPT doesn't follow the held-out gain.
- Kill criteria: no offline held-out gain over 3 points in a1 or cumulative a2 after step 3, or
  no in-server acceptance gain in step 4.
