## The idea
Pipeline each all-reduce at chunk level INSIDE the producer and consumer GEMMs, instead of splitting by tokens:
- **Producer** (out_proj / o_proj / down, row-parallel): the persistent W8A8 GEMM finishes output-column chunks in order, C = 4-8.
  When a chunk's tiles are done, a device counter triggers that chunk's D2H copy (stream wait-value on a device flag, graph-capturable).
- **Transport:** copy engines, raw bf16, duplex. The peer side waits per chunk on host-memory flags, then H2D.
  Uses T53's INTERFACE.md slot/flag layout where it fits.
- **Consumer** (gate_up / qkvz / attn qkv): a persistent kernel whose task queue holds per-chunk prep tasks (peer add + residual add
  + per-row ssq partial + W8A8 quant of the chunk's 128-groups) and GEMM tiles that walk K in chunk order. 1/rms is applied to the
  fp32 result in the epilogue (ruling 2026-11-11). Row ssq partials are summed in a fixed order, so it stays deterministic.
- A 3.8 MB AR at ~21 GB/s duplex is ~180 us of transfer, against a producer + consumer GEMM span of ~225-230 us at ~380 rows.
  Chunk latency (~20-40 us) is small at this scale.
