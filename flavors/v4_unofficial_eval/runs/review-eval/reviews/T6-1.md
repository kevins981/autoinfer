1. **#1 merits a microbenchmark; +1–3% is not established.** T5’s pricing is correct: `G1 + max(AR1,G2) + AR2`, compared with `G(full)+AR(full)`. If splitting preserves total GEMM time, savings are `min(AR1,G2)` **minus the extra collective overhead**. Rank waiting is already inside AR1; it supplies no additional overlap allowance. Include stream/event costs, concurrent slowdown, and automatic HMAR dispatch for chunks ≤96 rows.

2. **The ~82-row ridge is conditional on a contradicted ceiling.** Your own SQLite trace has a 1,849-row mixed step executing all 264 W8A8 GEMMs in **188.47 ms**. Using the model’s ~24.35 GFLOP/row gives **~239 useful TFLOP/s**, already above 165. Thus SPEED_OF_LIGHT’s 165 assumption needs correction; “130-row halves remain compute-bound, so rereads are free” is unproved. Also, the contract permits tensor-core accumulation within each 128-element block; it requires FP32 accumulation across blocks.

3. **Mixed-step calibration combines incompatible statistics.** Recomputing `steps.csv`: mixed rows have **median 258.5, mean 437.4**; periods have **median 119.0 ms, mean 147.3 ms**. The 421 µs NCCL figure is a call average, so subtracting a 205 µs microbenchmark at *260 rows* does not establish 216 µs of waiting. Only **26/50 mixed steps qualify at ≥192 rows**, accounting for 4.58/7.36 seconds of profiled mixed periods. Weight candidate savings by actual eligible shapes and durations. Scaling a short run’s step counts and multiplying medians does not establish the official mixed-time fraction.

4. **GDN traffic is mostly corrected in file 3, but files 2/4 remain inconsistent.** CUDA loads one accepted state and stores **three distinct snapshots**, even though it processes the rows together (`fused_gdn_decode_kernel.cu:174,323`). At 16 requests, baseline traffic is **4.83 GB**, not graph file 2’s 2.42 GB. L2-delayed writeback is plausible, but does not establish a 2.4–3.8 ms marginal latency interval. Capacity savings require allocator changes, not merely omitted stores.
