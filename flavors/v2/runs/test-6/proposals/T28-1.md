# T28-1: FP8 tensor cores at decode sizes: a small-M W8A8 GEMM that makes deeper exact speculation (k=3/4) pay

Team T28 (explore), leader-28. Champion T27-002, 967.3 tok/s (trial ~94 s). Bar: payoff if it works >= 1015.7 (+5%).
Split agreed with T29 (leader-29): T29 owns W8A8 GEMMs dispatched at M > 96 (mixed/prefill steps); T28 owns M <= 96
(decode shapes) and deeper speculation. One shared weight layout (checkpoint [N,K] e4m3 + fp32 [N/128,K/128] scales;
activations from per_token_group_quant_fp8), dispatch by M, so the two stack.

## The idea
Decode (60% of wall, KB77) is byte-bound: 12.25 GB of weights per rank per step, 13.0 ms of GEMM at M=48 in situ (KB79).
The only way past a byte floor is more accepted tokens per weight pass, i.e. deeper speculation. That was tried (T5, KB63)
and lost: fixed k=3 raised E 2.345 -> 2.69 (+15%) but the step grew 25.22 -> 29.55 ms (+17%), -3.5% e2e (T5-004). The
step growth decomposes (T5-003/006, KB63) into: **Marlin's 3->4 m-block tensor cliff at M=64, +3.0-3.4 ms** (bf16 MMA at
68% of HMMA peak is already at the tensor roof at M=48, KB66/KB69); 3rd draft step +0.8; AR +0.6; GDN/attn/norm +0.2.
The cliff is the killer, and it is a bf16-tensor limit. The human's 2026-11-09 ruling allows the checkpoint's W8A8 scheme,
and e4m3 MMA runs at 2x the bf16 rate (KB69: 291 vs 146 TF on GPU0). Tensor time at M=64 becomes 64 x 24.4 GFLOP / 291 TF
= 5.4 ms, far under the 12.9 ms byte floor: an FP8 W8A8 GEMM at M=64 should cost about what Marlin costs at M=48, and
the cliff disappears. With no dequant and half the activation bytes it may also beat Marlin's warm (L2 prefetch) arm,
which four teams (T8/T17/T24/T26) could not do with bf16-dequant kernels (KB68/KB81/KB83).

## Measured so far
- T29's microbench (agents/leader-29.w1/mb/summary.md, jobs 190/191; GPU0 cold read-flush, per-forward linear totals):
  Marlin M=48 16.35 ms, M=64 17.69 (+1.34 cold; in situ the cliff is +3.0-3.4, T5); stock Triton block W8A8 incl. quant:
  M=48 16.53, M=64 17.27, M=96 21.34. So even the stock Triton kernel removes most of the M=64 cliff (+0.9 vs Marlin M=48).
  Its quant launches cost ~3 us x ~320 GEMMs = ~0.9 ms/forward (launch-bound): fusing quant into producers matters at decode.
- E at k=3: 2.69 (T5-004), 2.67 (KB20, T1-015). The champion's screened draft head leaves acceptance unchanged (KB84).
- k=3 memory: 16 requests fit at KV 5.75 GiB with the champion's 1536 grid, 128K passes (T5-004); two-slot GDN state is
  bitwise and frees more for k=4 (T5-005, ported to the champion in T25-003).

1. **Kill test (seed, running):** a custom small-M FP8 W8A8 CUDA kernel on the shared layout (register-direct or cp.async B
   stream, e4m3 mma.sync m16n8k32, fp32 rescale per 128-K block, split-K for N=5120 shapes). Success line: M=64 per-forward
   total <= Marlin's M=48 total, same job. Fallback if it misses: T29's tuned Triton configs at M<=96 (+0.9 ms/forward).
2. **In-situ k=3 measurement (seed):** T29's env-gated all-M W8A8 path (T29_W8A8) + num_speculative_tokens 3 + KV budget as
   T5-004, vs the champion, same job: step time and E in situ. This measures the forecast's main part before the grant is spent.
3. **Integrate:** the kernel as the M<=96 branch of the shared dispatch; fused activation quant (shared with T29's step B);
   k=3 serve args; 128K test; GSM8K-1319 same-job A/B vs the bf16-activation reference (the ruling's condition; shared
   with T29 where the code is the same); same-job ABBA vs the champion; official eval.
4. **Deeper:** k=4 at M=80 (tensor 6.7 ms, still under the byte floor) with two-slot GDN state (T25-003) for memory;
   retune the decode L2 prefetch (T10's coupling map: a GEMM that is no longer tensor-bound can cash the full prefetch).
