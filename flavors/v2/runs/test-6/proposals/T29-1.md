# T29-1: FP8 tensor cores for mixed/prefill steps (checkpoint W8A8, per-token-group-128)

Team T29 (explore), leader-29. Champion T27-002, 967.3 tok/s. Bar: payoff if it works >= 1015.7 (+5%).
Split agreed with T28: T29 owns W8A8 GEMMs at M > 96 (mixed and prefill steps), T28 owns decode sizes
(M <= 96) and deeper speculation. Both dispatch by M, so the two stack.

## Why this is new
Every earlier team worked under the rule that FP8-MMA is inexact (KB69). The human's 2026-11-09 ruling now allows the
checkpoint's own W8A8 scheme. Mixed steps are 40% of wall (KB77), and their excess over decode is mostly Marlin running
at the **bf16** tensor roof (22.0 of 33.7 s/trial, T19). e4m3 MMA runs at 2x the bf16 rate (KB69: 291 vs 146 TF on
GPU0). This is the "unused capability" the run could not touch before.

## Measured so far (jobs 190, 191; agents/leader-29.w1/mb/NOTES.md, summary.md)
- **Setup:**
  - Real checkpoint weights, all six FP8 linear shapes per rank, 256 linears per forward.
  - GPU0 (the pacing GPU), cold with a READ flush, medians.
  - Marlin is called exactly as the champion calls it (T6 policy).
  - W8A8 = vLLM's per_token_group_quant_fp8 + Triton `_w8a8_triton_block_scaled_mm` with the best of 16 configs.
- **Exactness:** the Triton kernel does `acc += dot(a,b) * a_s * b_s` per BLOCK_K=128 with an fp32 accumulator; the PTX
  is `mma.sync.m16n8k32...e4m3.e4m3.f32`. This complies with the ruling (FP8 accumulator only within each 128-K block).
  BLOCK_K must stay 128 (asserted).
- **Per forward, ms (Marlin -> W8A8 incl. quant):**

## Roadmap
1. **(A) In-server W8A8, Python-only.** Env-gated, per-M config table, graph-safe.
   - Same-job ABBA vs the champion.
   - GSM8K-1319 A/B in one job vs a bf16-activation reference (the ruling's condition).
   - 128K test (memory changes: no Marlin workspace).
   - Record -> official eval.
2. **(B) Fuse the activation quant into its producers.** RMSNorm -> quant, and SiLU-mul -> quant; vLLM already has
   `silu_mul_per_token_group_quant_fp8` and norm-quant fusion hooks. Removes the measured quant gap (1-5 ms/forward at
   mixed M) and the bf16 round trip. I'll share it with T28 if they need the same quant.
3. **(C) A faster block-scaled FP8 GEMM at mixed M.** Triton is 20-27% slower than cuBLASLt's FP8 speed at M>=192.
   Options: tuned Triton (split-K / stream-K, larger tiles, persistent), or a CUTLASS sm89 kernel with per-128-K
   promotion in the mainloop. Exactness condition unchanged.
4. **(D) Re-tune around the new mixed step.** With cheaper mixed GEMMs, revisit:
   - the token-split threshold and the prefill graph grid (KB33/KB44 padding);
   - the max batched tokens;
   - GPU0 skew, which should shrink because the compute share falls.
