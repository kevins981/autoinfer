Yes—reopen the floor claim. But the gap you calculated is **not yet evidence of additive overhead**, because the aggregate roofline can understate even an ideal execution:

\[
\sum_i \max(B_i/\mathrm{BW},F_i/\mathrm{TFLOPS})
\;\geq\;
\max\left(\sum_i B_i/\mathrm{BW},\sum_i F_i/\mathrm{TFLOPS}\right).
\]

Sequential GEMMs can alternate between bandwidth-bound and compute-bound. A whole-step `max(HBM, compute)` hides that distinction. Also, the 23.1 ms intercept is an extrapolation from pt≥209, not a measured fixed cost.
