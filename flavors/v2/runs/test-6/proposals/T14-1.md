Team T14 (explore), leader-14. Champion reference: T9-005, 948.9 tok/s. Bar: payoff if it works >= 996.3 (+5%).

## Why here
- Decode steps (about 62% of wall) sit on coupled floors: HBM, bf16 tensor and PCIe. T2/T4/T8/T10/T11/T13 measured this, and I don't contest it.
- The non-decode part has measured excess and no owner. The config has no exploit team, and every explore team that sized these items handed them off as "exploit-sized".
  - Mixed steps are about 31% of wall (T2-006, T7).
  - Slot dead time (TTFT and drain) is about 8% (T10's replay).
- T9-005 took the large-step AR. What is left is mostly GEMM M-handling and glue, not communication.

## Where the excess is (measured)
Source: my CPU re-analysis of T2-006's nsys trace (champion T1-042; Marlin is unchanged in T9-005). Script: agents/leader-14/marlin_steps.py.
- Summed rank-0 Marlin time per mixed step, against max(R x 0.183 ms/row compute roof on GPU0, 12.7 ms HBM floor), scaled x6.25 to a 400-prompt trial:

| R (rows) | excess per trial | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| < 256 | 1.8 s | padded sizes 112/160 pay 2 launches per GEMM; R=102-112 runs 33 ms vs a ~20.5 ms roof |
| 256-600 | 1.6 s (summed) | |
| >= 600 | 3.5 s (summed) | |

- Steps with an odd-32 size show 716 Marlin launches instead of 456, and cost ~+20 ms per large step (e.g. R=776: 197 ms vs R=831: 177 ms).
- Large-M Marlin runs 0.20-0.25 ms/row in situ, against 0.183 cold (T2: "prefill Marlin at ~85% of its microbench rate"). Nobody has explained this.
- Caveat: for R>=256 the summed time overstates the critical path, because the two token-split chunks partly overlap on two streams. T2's critical-path median for pt>=400 is 157.7 ms vs a 142 ms roof (+11%).
- The non-GEMM excess in mixed steps (T2-006 medians):
  - in-span idle: 1.7 / 4.9 / 4.7 ms per small / mid / large step;
  - norm/act/elementwise: 3.0-5.5 ms;
  - DMA-AR combine add_kernel 3.6 ms per prefill step;
  - ~1100 aten glue launches per mixed step.

## Roadmap
**M1. Marlin M-handling for every M>48 in mixed steps.**
- Port T6-004's exact policy onto T9-005 (exact to <= 1 ulp, bitwise at M<=48). It does parallel slabs in one launch, slab-inner tile order and masked tails.
- Microbench (T6-004, M<=191): -21..-35% cold at M=72-120; M=96 at 1.02x roof.
- Extend it to M>=192 and to the token-split chunk sizes:
  - masked single launch (the T1-032 idea);
  - slab-inner order wherever 64-row slabs are HBM-bound. A 64-row slab is 11.7 ms of compute against 12.7 ms of weight stream.
- Diagnose the in-situ vs cold gap at large M.
- Gate G1 (seed job, running now as W1-1): in-situ nsys A/B in one job must show >= 1.5 s/trial less Marlin critical-path time in mixed steps, with decode unchanged.

**M2. Mixed-step glue** (T2-006's list, none of it built):
- fuse the DMA-AR combine add (dma_staged_ar.cu:61) into the following FusedAddRMSNorm;
- replace the GDN index_select/gather glue with slices (qwen_gdn_linear_attn.py:1878-1881, 1954-1955);
- act_and_mul runs at ~8x its byte floor: fuse it or fix it;
- shrink the in-span idle (eager launch gaps).
- Gate G2: >= 0.7 s/trial in an nsys A/B.

**M3. Admission lag.**
- Measure arrival -> first scheduled step -> first token per request.
- If a newly arrived request waits an extra async-scheduled step, admit it into the next step. Generic, not keyed to c=16.
- T10's replay puts ~115 ms/request of TTFT above a ~105 ms floor.
- Gate G3: mean TTFT -20 ms or better, with no throughput loss.

**M4. Stack and verify.**
- One workspace with every item behind switches.
- Same-job ABBA, 400 prompts, interleaved fresh starts, against T9-005.
- GSM8K-50 smoke and the 128K test if memory moved. Then official.

Each item is filed as its own record with its own nsys A/B. Items that fail their gate are dropped from the stack.
