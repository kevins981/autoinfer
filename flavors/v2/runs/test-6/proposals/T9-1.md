## The measured premise (bank/T9-001, job 69, ~3.8 GPU-min)
- Setup: 2-process microbench with both processes bound to node 0.
  - Proof: all 400 MB of /dev/shm slots are on N0 in both ranks.
  - Real FP8 Marlin, per-rank GDN-layer shapes, 8-layer CUDA-graph chain.
- Copy rates with node-0 slots:
  - copies alone: D2H/H2D 20-24 GB/s, also with both GPUs copying at once;
  - T7's 14 GB/s does not reproduce. Far-node runs were not re-run, so that cause for KB65 is likely, not proven.
- Ping-pong P:
  - Two token chunks, each running its whole layer chain on its own stream.
  - Every AR goes over the copy engines: D2H, doorbell, peer H2D, add.
  - Chunk B's core waits on chunk A's core, standing in for the GDN state dependency.

- Two cautions:
  - **The core is a sleep kernel and my 90 us/128 rows overstates real GDN at large R.** T2-006 gives GDN 177 us/layer at R~820, plus ~85 us/layer of norms and activations. Truth lies between the core=0 and core=90 rows, so I interpolate below.

## Roadmap
1. **M1, real-core microbench (<= 6 GPU-min).**
   - Replace the sleep core with the real GDN chunked-prefill kernel (and attention for 1 of 4 layers) at R=256/384/768/1024.
   - Gate: (S2-P)/(S2-N) >= 50% at R=384 and >= 75% at R>=768 on rank 0. Kill if < 30% at R=384.
2. **M2, in-server ping-pong for steps with R >= 256 (~20 GPU-min).**
   - Turn the champion's `_qwen3_5_split_chain` (join per layer at the mixer core) into a no-join schedule:
     - each chunk runs qkvz, GDN/attention core, out_proj, AR, norm, MLP and AR on its own stream;
     - chunk B's core waits on chunk A's core (GDN initial-state handoff; attention reads chunk A's KV);
     - both ARs go on the DMA lane.
   - Chunk boundaries stay 64-aligned, as in the champion, so GEMM rounding is unchanged. Checks:
     - AR sum order kept, bitwise to the champion;
     - GDN two-call handoff checked bitwise or max-abs vs one call;
     - gsm8k 50 and acceptance.
   - Gate: same-job interleaved ABBA vs T1-042 (3 starts each) >= +2.5%, every B above every A.
3. **M3, extend to R = 128-255 (~10 GPU-min).**
   - Chunk-size and threshold policy, and an uneven split (bigger chunk A) to cut the split cost.
   - Gate: net positive in the same job; otherwise keep the 256 threshold.
4. **M4, official eval (~20 GPU-min)** when M2/M3 beat T1-042 in every same-job pair.
5. **Optional, only after a manager ruling:**
   - Shift MLP neurons to GPU1 in compute-bound steps (GPU0 clock lock, KB8), needing ~0.6 GB/rank of duplicated FP8 slice and a smaller KV budget with the 128K test.
   - I'll ask before any work on it, and none of it is counted below.
