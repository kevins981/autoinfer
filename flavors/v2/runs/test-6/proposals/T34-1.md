# T34-1 (leader-34, explore): mixed-step all-reduce at its PCIe duplex floor (+ codec, + waves)

Bar: payoff-if-it-works >= +5% over T31-003 (1088.9 -> 1143.3). GPU used: 5.2 of the 20 seed (plus 4.0 lost in crashed job 345); balance 14.8.
Record: bank/T34-001 (stage 0). Notes: local_kb/leader-34.md. Sparring: T34-1.

## Direction
In big mixed steps the champion's copy-engine (DMA) AR is fully exposed: nothing computes beside it (leader-30.w1/attr RESULT §1-3). Exposed mixed-step AR is ~4.0 s of ~42 s per 200 prompts (T32-5).
Nobody had measured this AR against its own transport floor. The champion's DMA chain (D2H -> flag -> H2D -> add kernel) only overlaps the two directions mid-message.
- **H (measured, stage 0):** hybrid AR. CE D2H of own partial in 8 pieces + per-piece flag. ONE thread polls the peer's host flags (nanosleep back-off) and publishes them to a device word. 16 reader CTAs read the peer's piece straight from pinned host memory and add (same bf16(f32+f32) as today).
- **C:** lossless 0.78 block-exponent codec (hostar's) on the wire.
- **F:** add + RMSNorm + FP8 quant fused into the reader's epilogue.
- **W:** T30/T32's layer-pipelined waves (asset T32_GDN_CAP) on top. That scheme was capped exactly by the AR chain latency; H's first piece is usable after ~80 us.

## Roadmap with gates
1. (~15 GPU-min) Same-job transport A/B including the champion DMA arm (with a safe wait-value protocol) at R=128..1100. Then integrate H into the DMA lane of the token split with a real flag/ack protocol (no bench re-arm). Bitwise vs champion on served dumps. Same-job ABBA (abba.sh). **Gate: all-B > all-A, >= +1.0%.** If not, close.
2. (~20) Codec on the hybrid wire + fused epilogue. Gate: transport <= 560 us at 1100 in the microbench; ABBA >= +2% cumulative.
3. (~25) Waves (T32_GDN_CAP + T30 scheduler) on H. Gate: big-step span closer to the AR-off floor than T30's replay.
4. (~10) 128K check, official eval.
Exactness: H and W are bitwise to the champion (same add math, same kernels). C is lossless. F must be bitwise vs the unfused path (KB92 rules), so no new GSM8K A/B is needed.
Safety: job 345's crash cost a server reboot. Benches use bounded device polls only, pointer-lifetime asserts, a smoke stage before timing, and os._exit on error.
