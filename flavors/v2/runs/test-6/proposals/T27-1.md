# T27-1: cheap deep drafting + confidence-budgeted verification at M=48

Team T27 (explore), leader-27. Champion T14-004, 956.6 tok/s. Bar: payoff if it works >= 1004.4 (+5%).

## The idea, and what is new versus T3/T5
Decode steps are ~60% of wall time. Each decode step costs ~25.3 ms whatever the rows below M=48 (KB14, KB77), and every
part of that step sits near a measured floor. So the lever is **accepted tokens per step (E) at a fixed M=48**.

T3 and T5 built confidence-budgeted verification: draft K_MAX tokens and verify only the best B = 2 x num_reqs slots,
ranked by draft confidence. It is exact, because verification is unchanged. They stopped at about +1.7% (timer model)
for three reasons, and this proposal attacks each one:
1. **Each extra draft step costs 0.80 ms**, which eats most of the E gain. Of that, the FP8 draft lm_head is 306 us
   (T23-001: 1.16x its byte roof, so it is not slack, it is bytes).
   **New:** the drafter only proposes tokens, so the draft lm_head may be screened. A PCA screen of the drafter's own
   hidden states (r=512) picks top-64 candidates, then the exact FP8 draft logits are computed for those 64 only.
   That reads ~25-50 MB/rank instead of 252 MB. Draft tokens change only on screen misses (measured below).
2. **The in-server run lost on mixed steps** (T5-006: -4.8% measured vs +1.7% timer model; ITL mean +9.5%, TTFT +17%).
   The worker's suspects (WRAPUP.md) are all plumbing: the adaptive branch skips the uniform-length GDN shortcut, and
   mixed steps fall to piecewise/eager under the adaptive cudagraph mode.
   **New:** use the budget on decode-only steps only. Mixed steps keep the champion's stock k=2 path, which is still
   exact: the extra drafts are simply not verified.
3. **In-situ E fell short of the sim** (+5.6% vs +8.1%). The cause has not been examined. I will log per-step budgets
   and acceptance and compare them with the sim, on the same data.

Everything else is reused: the two-slot GDN (T5-005, ported onto the champion by T25-003), the in-graph confidence
kernel and adaptive-verify plumbing (T5-006), and the iso-calibrated ranker and simulator (T3-005/006, sim_budget2.py).

## Measured so far (CPU only, 0 GPU-min): screen recall
Script: agents/leader-27/lr/recall.py, recall2.py.
- Data: offline MTP (T3-001's vLLM-faithful model) on 8 held-out self-generated sequences, giving 7,362 draft
  positions (steps 1 and 2).
- The exact draft argmax is taken over the first 98,304 lm_head rows (bf16 proxy for the FP8 head).
- **Data-free SVD of the lm_head fails:** recall@64 is 0.38-0.69 for r = 64-512.
- **PCA of the drafter's hidden states works.** Fit on half the positions, test on the other half:

  | r | recall@32 | recall@64 | recall@128 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 256 | 0.970 | 0.982 | 0.991 |
  | 512 | 0.989 | **0.994** | 0.996 |
  | 1024 | 0.996 | **0.998** | 0.999 |

  A miss changes the draft in 0.2-0.6% of positions, and those are mostly low-confidence ones. Expected acceptance
  loss is < 0.3%. Still to do (CPU): fit on train sequences only, test on held-out, measure acceptance directly.
