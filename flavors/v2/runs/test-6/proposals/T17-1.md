# T17-1: a roofline decode GEMM for M<=64, cashed through k=3 speculation

Team T17 (explore), leader-17. Champion: T14-004, 956.6 tok/s. Bar: payoff if it works >= 1004.4.

## Idea
Tokens per decode step are the remaining multiplier: the step is ~80% fixed cost (1 req 20.3 ms, 16 req 25.5 ms, T14),
and k=3 raises E by +16% (2.33 -> 2.69-2.73, T5-004, T1-008) with memory already solved (two-slot GDN, T5-005, bitwise,
128K pass). k=3 lost only because Marlin steps from 3 to 4 m-blocks at M=49: +2.1 ms cold, +3.0-3.4 ms in situ
(T5-002/003/006). T5's own conclusion: a bar-clearing speculation gain needs "a single pass at M<=64 with no step from
3 to 4 m-blocks (a kernel, not a config)". Nobody built it:
- T8 built a 2-warp GEMM for **M<=48**, where Marlin is already near the HBM roof cold, and killed it at M1 on a load
  path that sustained only 0.75 TB/s with 1 KB strided pieces, a lockstep barrier per stage, ~8 us startup and ~9 us
  owner-reduce tail (T8-003). Its loop alone kept 79% of HMMA peak.
- T6 fixed Marlin for M=65-191 (slabs), not 49-64 (only -3%).

**Where the headroom is (measured, T6-001, cold, per-forward sum, GPU0):** HBM roof 12.74 ms; tensor at the measured
133 TF bf16 rate 0.183 ms/row. So max(mem, compute) is 12.74 ms at M=48 *and* at M=64 (11.7 ms compute). Marlin:
15.54 ms at M=48 (1.22x roof), 17.65 ms at M=64 (1.39x roof). At M=64 the weight byte carries 128 FLOP, so the GEMM sits
exactly at the ridge: a kernel that overlaps stream and math (time = max, not ~sum) makes M=64 cost what M=48 should.

## Roadmap
- **M0 (seed, <= 5 GPU-min): premise kernel.** Standalone kernel on Marlin's repacked FP8 layout + scales (no extra
  memory; dequant bit-identical to Marlin, bf16(w x s); fp32 accumulate), M=64 and M=48, gate_up and qkvz shapes.
  Design vs T8: 4 m16 tiles per dequantized B fragment; >= 4 KB contiguous chunks per stage (CTA n-width 256), >= 48 KB
  in flight per SM; 3-4 warps/scheduler; no lockstep per-stage barrier for all warps; fixed overheads (startup, split-K
  tail) <= 5 us via stream-K style partitioning. Same-job Marlin reference on a built T14-004 workspace.
  **Gate:** cold gate_up at M=64 <= 105 us (Marlin ~126, roof ~93) and M=48 <= Marlin M=48, max diff <= 1 bf16 ulp.
  **Kill:** two design iterations > 115 us at M=64.
- **M1 (~15 min): all decode shapes** (GDN in/out, attn qkv/o, gate_up, down, MTP layer), M=1..64, deterministic.
  16-layer chain with the champion's hostar + L2 prefetch, vs Marlin. Gate: chain M=64 <= Marlin chain M=48.
- **M2 (~25 min): in-server at k=2.** Dispatch decode Marlin calls (M<=64) to the kernel inside the decode graphs;
  mixed/prefill keep Marlin + T6 policy. nsys anatomy, same-job ABBA vs T14-004, gsm8k 50.
- **M3 (~30 min): k=3.** Port T5's two-slot GDN (ws t5-rb) onto the champion; k=3 + kernel; 128K request; ABBA vs
  T14-004. Optional: T5's budgeted verification (K_MAX=4 at M=64).
- **Official (~20 min)** when every same-job pair beats the champion.
