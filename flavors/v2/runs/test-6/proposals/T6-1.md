# T6-1: one weight stream for 49-191 rows (W8A16 GEMM), then use the decode slack

Team T6 (leader-6, worker leader-6.w1). Champion reference: T1-042, 942.2 tok/s. Bar: payoff if it works must be
at least +5%, i.e. >= 989 tok/s.

## The gap (measured)
- **Marlin vs roof per M** (T6 job 31; agents/leader-6.w1/mb1.py, adapted from T5-002's mb_marlin.py).
  - Setup: cold (L2-flushed), per-rank TP=2 shapes, sum over one forward (48 GDN + 16 attention layers), GPU 0.
  - Roofs: memory 12.74 ms (12.16 GB at 955 GB/s). Compute 0.183 ms/row at the 133 TF 4096^3 peak.
    Marlin itself reaches 120-133 TF at M=128/256, so that roof is reachable.

| M | 3 | 48 | 52-64 | 72 | 80 | 96 | 112 | 128 | 160 | 192 | 256 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marlin ms | 14.3 | 15.5 | 17.65 | 25.4 | 25.8 | 28.1 | 29.7 | 25.9 | 35.8 | 35.4 | 46.6 |
| max(mem, compute) roof ms | 12.7 | 12.7 | 12.7 | 13.2 | 14.6 | 17.6 | 20.5 | 23.4 | 29.3 | 35.1 | 46.9 |

- **Why the waste.** marlin_mm's host loop splits M into 64-row chunks and launches each remainder separately.
  Each launch streams all 12 GB of weights, because gate_up (89 MB) and down (45 MB) per rank exceed the 72 MB L2.
  - M=65-127 pays two streams; M=128 is cheaper than M=72-112. When two full slabs run in one launch, they share
    the weights through L2.
  - Within 49-64 rows, a thread_m_blocks=4 step adds +2.1 ms. Configs recover only 0.76 ms of it (T5-002, job 29).
- **Why KB44/T1-032 doesn't cover this.**
  - That fix merges the extra launch for non-64-multiple M into one masked launch. Within it, slabs are still
    serial 64-row passes, so the rows above 64 still trigger a second weight pass. The plateaus are per slab:
    in-server M=101..128 all take ~51 ms (T3-008 timers).
  - T1-032 is not in the champion anyway (KB45).
  - KB42's "≈ cuBLAS bf16" compared against bf16 weights (2x the bytes), not against a W8A16 roof.
  - KB28/39/49 concern M ≤ 48, which is not targeted here.
- **In-server consequence.**
  - A decode step at k=3 costs 26.32 ms at M=64, against 22.35 at M=48 (T5 job 28).
  - The rows from 48 up to the ridge (~75) are nearly free in GEMM terms, but are unusable today. KB14's M=80
    "cliff" is this same effect.

## Roadmap
1. **Host-side first (cheap).** Change marlin_mm's M split so the rows of one GEMM run as parallel slabs in a
   single launch (sharing L2):
   - for M=65-127, two slabs of ⌈M/32⌉·16 rows (e.g. 2x48 for M=96) instead of 64 + remainder;
   - for M=49-64, try 2x32 or 4x16 parallel slabs against thread_m_blocks=4.
   - Exact: same per-row math, only the tiling changes; at most fp32 reduction order. Checked bitwise/max-abs
     against the stock op.
   - **Gate G1** (microbench, ~1 GPU-min): M=72-112 ≤ 1.25x roof (from 1.45-1.93x), and M=64 ≤ 16.0 ms.
2. **If G1 is short:** a single-pass W8A16 kernel.
   - Either CUTLASS mixed-input upcast MMA (e4m3→bf16 converters exist; no ready GEMM, unverified), or Marlin with
     thread_m_blocks up to 8 and a narrower N tile.
   - Same gate. Multi-day; stop if it doesn't reach 1.25x within the second grant.
3. **In-server stack on T1-042** (same-job ABBA, 200 prompts). Mixed steps alone first, then hand the M=49-64
   result to T5, whose k=3 needs it (agreed split: T6 = GEMM, T5 = speculation policy and GDN-slot memory).
4. **Piggyback.** Chunked-prefill budget near the ridge (scheduler flag + a fixed-shape mixed graph). Only if
   step 3 shows in-server rows above 48 at ≤ 0.12 ms/row.
