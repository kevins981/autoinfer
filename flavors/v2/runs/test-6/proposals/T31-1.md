# Proposal T31-1 (leader-31, explore, slot A): an e4m3-MMA decode GEMM that cashes the all-reduce L2 prefetch

Champion at writing: T29-006, 1062.1 tok/s. Bar: 1115.2 (+5%).

## The idea in one paragraph
A decode step (77% of a trial: ~2690 steps x 24.5 ms) is GEMM 13.1 ms + host all-reduce 5.7 ms + ~5 ms of GDN/lm_head/drafter/glue (KB91). During each of the 137 all-reduce windows (~42 us) HBM is idle, and the champion already prefetches the next GEMM's weights into L2 from extra blocks of the AR kernel (KB25, host_staged_ar.cu, up to 88 MB per call). But Marlin cashes almost none of it: its L2-warm time is only ~7% below its cold time (gate_up 85.8 vs 92.5 us, KB76/KB81; qkvz 41.6 vs 55.3, KB79), because at M=48 it is bound by its bf16-dequant + smem/ldmatrix data path (~1 TB/s equivalent even from L2), not by HBM and not by the tensor cores. The checkpoint's own W8A8 scheme is allowed at any M (task_definition.md, 2026-11-09): e4m3 MMA runs at 2x the bf16 rate with no dequant (KB69: 291 TF on GPU0), so an FP8 decode GEMM with a register-direct weight path has a warm floor of ~30-35 us on gate_up (MMA 29 us at peak; ~120 MB of L2->SM traffic at ~4 TB/s) instead of 86, and the same cold floor as Marlin (HBM). The payoff is the prefetched part of every decode GEMM: up to ~5.7 GB of the 12.25 GB streamed per step.

## Why this is not a repeat of T8/T17/T24/T26/T28
- T8, T17, T24, T26 built bf16-MMA decode GEMMs: with bf16 MMA the gate_up tensor floor is 58.7 us and the dequant path is in the loop. T26's register-direct swap-AB loop reached 93% of HMMA peak (KB83), so register-direct weight streaming works; its full kernel lost because the weight stream fell to 0.7-1.0 TB/s, with per-CTA activation re-reads (~63 MB of L2 traffic per GEMM) as the main suspect. That is a design item (stage the activation operand once per CTA; wide N-slabs; split-K for skinny shapes), not a hardware limit.
- T28 built e4m3 MMA inside Marlin's own template (cp.async -> smem -> ldmatrix) and got only -7% warm at M=48 and at M=16 (ASSET.md table: 9.21 vs 9.89 ms/forward at M=16), i.e. the template's data path, not the MMA, was the bound. It also targeted k=3 (M=64), where the register spill at tmb=4 (KB94) and the AR/drafter costs (KB95) killed the payoff. This proposal keeps k=2, M=48, and replaces the data path.
- Nobody has combined e4m3 MMA + register-direct weights + a once-per-CTA activation operand, nor measured how much of the AR-window prefetch is actually L2-resident when the next GEMM starts (w2 is measuring both now).

## Roadmap
S1 (seed, in flight, ~11 GPU-min): (a) standalone prototype kernel on the champion's Marlin fp8 layout (T29's PI fragment trick, PI_SPEC.md; one weight copy; exact per-128-K fp32 promotion), cold/warm/prefetched vs Marlin bf16 and T28's e4m3 on both GPUs (w1, bank record T31-001); (b) the L2 budget: L2 read bandwidth vs working-set size after evict_last prefetch, MB resident per real AR window vs the AR stretch, Marlin chain reproduction of KB79 (w2, T31-002). Go/no-go: gate_up warm <= 55 us and >= 30 MB resident per window.
S2 (~25 GPU-min): integrate at M<=96 in T29's custom op (the documented plug-in branch of t29_marlin_fp8_linear), activation quant from T29's fused producers (silu->quant bitwise, Triton; norm + separate quant at M<256) plus two new small quant launches per layer (GDN core output, attention output); prefetch sites: AR#1 -> gate_up (first ~40 MB), AR#2 -> next layer's qkvz/attn_qkv (all), GDN/attention core -> gdn_out/attn_o (new prefetch site; stretch item). In-server bitwise check of the fused producers (T29_CAPTURE path), gsm8k-50 smoke, one nsys decode-step attribution, prefetch-byte tuning by same-job A/B.
S3 (~40 GPU-min): same-job ABBA vs T29-006 (abba.sh); GSM8K-1319 FP8-vs-bf16 A/B in one job (new FP8 code at decode: its own A/B is required); official eval. No memory change (no 128K test needed unless S2 adds buffers).
S4 (stretch, resync): k=3 on top (an MMA-bound GEMM pays little for 16 extra rows; the fixed AR+draft costs ~1.7-2 ms vs E +14%, KB95), down_proj prefetch during gate_up's tail, drafter linears in e4m3.

## Forecast
Payoff if it works (S1-S3 as planned, kernel at its warm floor, ~5.7 GB/step resident): decode GEMM phase 13.1 -> ~9.3 ms/step (prefetched bytes x (1/0.95 - 1/3 TB/s) = ~4.1 ms, minus ~0.4 ms of quant launches), decode step 24.5 -> ~20.8 ms (-15%), trial 85.9 -> ~76 s: **+12% over 1062.1 (~1190 tok/s)**. Small mixed steps (M<=96) gain a little more; not counted.
Central estimate: **+5%** (kernel reaches ~60% of the warm-floor gap, ~30 MB resident per window, some AR stretch, KB93-style in-situ transfer). Odds of the full payoff ~25%; odds of clearing +5% ~35%; odds of a null (kernel no better than Marlin in situ, like T28-008) ~40%.
