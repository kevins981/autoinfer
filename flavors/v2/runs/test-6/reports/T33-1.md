## What changed since T33-1
- The sparring partner challenged my T33-1 claim that decode is capped:
  - GEMM + AR take ~19 ms for 12.15 GB of weights per step, about 640 GB/s, against ~1 TB/s.
  - T31's "gate_up wall" ablation shows ~sum, not max, of the weight-stream (56-63 us) and MMA (53 us) phases. Both ablation builds use the same registers and residency.
- So I tested a new combination:
  - (1) a decode GEMM in which the HBM weight stream and the MMA run concurrently;
  - (2) card 13's lossless weight code on top, which should pay once the kernel is stream-bound.

## Why it doesn't reach the bar
- **Overlap alone works but is small.**
  - In situ the kernel is stream-bound plus ~10 us of handoff/tail.
  - Overlapped gate_up + down saves ~8 us/layer vs champion-like, ~0.5 ms/step, about +1.5% e2e. This assumes decode is ~60-65% of wall, which is not measured.
- **Fewer bytes would pay** (free-decode stand-in: -27..-30 us/layer). But every exact codec we built loses its saving exactly where the champion's AR prefetch makes weights L2-resident: decoding becomes the bottleneck there.
  - The escape-patching decoder costs ~22 us/call.
  - Exact correction of escapes costs far more.
  - Escape-free windows need 4-bit codes, so no saving.
- **Even with free decode**, the measured 0.8925 code gives ~+4.4-4.8% e2e: below the bar unless the decode share is higher than assumed.
- **Classification (narrowed by the sparring partner):** the tested exact codecs fail in the prefetched regime. That is not a proven hardware limit.
  - An ideal entropy code (0.821) with free decode would be ~+7% on paper.
  - Denser codes decode more expensively, not less, and none is tested.
  - No >= 5% direction has been demonstrated, and I have no measured lead toward one.

## Recommendation
- **Close T33.**
- **The overlapped decode GEMM is an exploit-sized item:** ~+1-1.5% e2e est.; gate_up -6 us/layer in situ, measured.
  - Productising needs a second weight copy in the stage-major layout (~1.3 GiB/rank for gate_up; check against KV slack), or replacing Marlin on the decode path.
  - It also needs per-M configs for M <= 96.
  - Then an FP8 GSM8K A/B (new FP8 GEMM kernel) and an ABBA.
  - Details: agents/leader-33.w1/ASSET.md.
- **For any later card-13 attempt:** the cost to beat is decode+MMA <= ~60 us for gate_up at M=48 with ~25-50 MB L2-resident. The decode-free chain value is in T33-004.

## KB candidates
1. A decode GEMM with warp-specialized weight stream + e4m3 MMA reaches max(stream, MMA), not sum (synthetic, 4+12 warps/SM). With a real smem handoff it is stream-bound + ~10 us. In the chain with the champion's hint prefetch, gate_up is 81.6 vs Marlin 88.0 us; down gains nothing (t31b already ~90% of the cold floor). Refines KB98: the gate_up "wall" was the kernel's phase serialization, but removing it buys only ~6 us/layer in situ. (T33-002..004, T33-007)
2. Lossless coding of the FP8 weights inside the decode GEMM: 0.8925 of bytes on the real checkpoint, bit-exact, wins cold (-7%) but loses when weights are L2-prefetched (decode+MMA floor 85 vs 63 us). Exact correction of escapes costs more. Free-decode ceiling at 0.875x bytes: -27..-30 us/layer. (T33-001, T33-005, T33-006)
3. Measurement hazard: evict_last lines from an L2-prefetch rep survive a plain read flush. Call cudaCtxResetPersistingL2Cache() before each cold rep, or "cold" reads ~15% fast. (T33-003)
