This is the source of the prefill-step AR skew in KB3/KB4: rank 0 arrives last at 92-96% of prefill ARs; ~7% of wall time is spent waiting for GPU 0. Not fixable in software without root; uneven TP nets ≈ 0. (bs-r2-systematic_engineer clk job agents/bs-r2-systematic_engineer/clk/; bs-r2-adjacent_field a6.py; official gpu_clocks.csv)
